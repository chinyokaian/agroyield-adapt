         Structure of DAI research paper 1: Survey Paper (build-up or formulation)

The survey paper should address the following:

     Critically study and analyse the base project (aka systematic literature review of
         area of study)

     Conduct a literature review of 10 to 22 foundational papers relevant to a specific
         scope recommended by the base project

     Include your own commentary on the significance of the approach and the solutions
         presented in each paper with respect to the base project

     Provide a critical assessment of the work that has been done in the base project
     Include a discussion on future research directions
     Meet IEEE standards (Referencing Style & Formatting)
     Meet UZ DAI assessment standards (5-8 pages/~4000 words)

The typical structure of the paper includes the following sections:

    1. Title:
         A descriptive title ("Survey of ...") and the student name and student number on a
         separate title page.

    2. Abstract:
         An abstract which briefly describes the entire paper. This should be written last.

    3. Introduction:
         An introduction that summarises what the paper presents without expecting the
         reader to have already read the paper. This should be written second last.

    4. Body of paper/Literature Review
         A problem description which explains the problem and motivates interest in it. The
         actual survey with critical analysis of each paper presented and the liberal use of
         figures to aid in the exposition. This clearly highlights the flaws and challenges in the
         base project as well as potential solutions or improvements to the aforementioned
         flaws with the aid of the related literature as backing for the feasibility of proposed
         solutions and improvements.

    5. Conclusion/Proposed or Future Work:
         A conclusion which summarises the paper (now assuming the reader has read it) and
         which describes possible directions for future research on the base project.

    6. References:
         A detailed bibliography presented in an easy to read and consistent format, following
         the IEEE Reference style.
