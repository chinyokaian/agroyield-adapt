    DAI ASSESSMENT GUIDELINES
                         for

               Capstone Projects

                 PHASE - I (Odd Semester Part 3)
Zeroth Review (NO Marks to be awarded)

Objectives
     To Assess the Problem Statement of the Project work prepared by the Student.
     To Assess the Fundamental knowledge of the candidate in the area of work.

Assessment Guidelines
     The project idea can be approved or rejected based on the originality of the work and the
         Student's confidence in completing the project.
     Area of study and up to 3 topics can be considered.

                PHASE - II (Even Semester Part 3)
PROJECT PROPOSAL

Objectives
The candidate is expected to complete the following activities:

     Background
     Study of Base Paper and Problem Formulation (Problem Statement)

                                                                                                                              1
     Survey of literature relevant to the area of the work (Pre-literature review)
     Motivation of work
     Objectives
     Justification
     Proposed Solution to the Problem identified above (Student's own contribution).

Review Guidelines
The candidate shall submit a project proposal document and give a Powerpoint Presentation of the
progress of work, thus far, to the panel of reviewers for duration of 10 minutes. The presentation is
expected to contain the following information in the order specified:

     Logical flow of ideas inspired from the survey which leads to the problem Statement
     Clear and Concise description of the problem statement
     Pros and Cons of existing solutions to the problem, if any.

                PHASE - III (Odd Semester Part 4)
CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT & EVALUATION REPORT

Review - 1 (Gap Analysis)

Objectives
The candidate is expected to complete the following work:

     Chapter 1 adoption from proposal
     Chapter 2 adoption from pre-literature review
     Formulation of the solution
     Identification of tools to carry out experiments and test the project outcome.
     Identification of development and test environment
     First draft of the Formulated Solution
     First draft of Development, Test Procedures
     Initial Analysis of the Formulated Solution using UML diagrams

                                                                                                                              2
     Expected Outcome.

Review Guidelines
The candidate shall give a Powerpoint Presentation of the progress of work, thus far, to the panel of
reviewers for a duration of 10 minutes. The presentation is expected to contain the following
information in the order specified:

     A Quick Recap of Phase II (Proposal), highlighting the problem statement and formulated
         solution

     Clear Discussion of the solution
     Information about the development and test environment needed
     Experimental procedures (Highlight the methodologies involved to conduct experiments)
     Test procedures (highlight the different scenarios of test and the outcome), Data Collection etc
     Analysis shall highlight the pros and cons of the formulated solution, impact of the outcome
     Highlight the progress in identification of the development and test environment.

Review - 2 (Design)

Objectives
The candidate is expected to have implemented the following:

     Methodology adoption (Chapter 3)
     Final Design of the Formulated Solution
     Final Development, Test Procedures and Data Collection
     Completion of Analysis of the Formulated Solution using UML diagrams
     Acquiring development and test environment
     First draft of the research survey paper.

Review Guidelines
The candidate shall submit two documents to the supervisor; the draft research paper and the concept
development and evaluation report. The candidate will also give a PowerPoint Presentation of the

progress of work, thus far, to the panel of reviewers for duration of 10 minutes. The presentation is
expected to contain the following information in the order specified:

     A Quick Recap of Review 2 (Chapters 1 & 2), highlighting the problem statement and

     Design may contain mathematical model of formulated solution, algorithm design, protocol

     Experimental procedures (Highlight the methodologies involved to conduct experiments), Test

                PHASE -IV (Even Semester Part 4)
       PROTOTYPE/MODEL DEVELOPMENT AND

                      EVALUATION REPORT

Review - 3

Objectives

The candidate is expected to have completed the following stages:
     Implementation of the work contained in the base paper
     Formulation of proposed solution (Enhancement).
         (It is expected that the student after implementation of the work contained in the base
         paper, will identify enhancements to the research ideas contained in the base paper as
         selected for the project.)
     Analysis of the Results, if any
     First draft of technical research paper for submission (library, conferences or journals)

Review Guidelines

The candidate shall give a PowerPoint Presentation of the progress of work, thus far, to the panel of

                                                                                                                              4
reviewers for duration of 10 minutes. The presentation is expected to contain the following information
in the order specified:

     A quick recap of phase III (Chapters 1 to 3)
     Plan for implementation of proposed enhancement work and the expected outcome
     Highlight the implementation done, thus far, with substantial proofs (screenshots, result data,

         graphs etc.)
     Analysis of the results
     Review of the draft project report.
     Update on the initial draft technical paper.

Review - 4 (Final Viva voce/Oral presentation of project)

Objectives

The candidate is expected to present on the following:
     Draft outline of the Research/Project.
     Complete implementation of the enhanced project work.
     Project Demonstration.

Review Guidelines
     The candidate shall present on the completed research project to the department. The candidate
         shall also give a demo of their completed project work, with substantial proofs of the study,
         experiments, data collection and future enhancements.
     Reviewers evaluate the mode of conduct of experiments and the results produced.
     Submission of the final draft technical paper, for library or journal publication.

SUBMISSION OF FINAL WRITTEN PROJECT REPORT

Objectives
     Assessment of final draft or capstone project report.
     Submission of survey and technical paper as annexes to final project report.
